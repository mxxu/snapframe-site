Snapframe

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Snapframe does not collect, transmit, or sell your screenshots or browsing data. Screenshots are stored only on your own computer.

What Snapframe stores, and where

Screenshots. Every capture is saved in your browser's local IndexedDB storage on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting a screenshot, or using “Delete all history” in Settings, removes it permanently.

Preferences. Capture settings are stored in your browser's local extension storage on your device.

What leaves your device

Snapframe makes exactly one kind of network request: purchase status checks, and only if you purchase Snapframe Pro.

When you buy Pro or check your purchase status, Snapframe sends a randomly generated device ID (and, when activating a purchase made elsewhere, the order ID from your email) to:

  1. our license server, hosted on Cloudflare Workers, and
  2. Waffo Pancake, our payment provider and merchant of record.

Waffo stores your purchase information (email address, order details) as the seller of record, and handles sales tax in your jurisdiction. The device ID is a random value generated by Snapframe; it is not derived from your hardware and cannot be used to identify you outside the license system.

What Snapframe does not do

  • It does not read or transmit the content of the pages you capture.
  • It does not track your browsing history.
  • It uses no analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • It serves no advertisements.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Permissions and why they are needed

<all_urls> Required to capture screenshots of whatever page you are viewing. Snapframe only reads the page when you explicitly trigger a capture.
activeTab Lets the toolbar and shortcuts capture the current tab.
scripting Injects the capture and region-selection overlay into the page you invoke it on.
downloads Saves your screenshots to your Downloads folder.
clipboardWrite Copies a screenshot to your clipboard when you choose Copy.
contextMenus Adds the Snapframe submenu to the right-click menu.
storage Stores your preferences and license state locally.
offscreen Composes full-page screenshots on a canvas, where the browser requires a document context.

Not affiliated with Chrome

Snapframe is an independent extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC.
